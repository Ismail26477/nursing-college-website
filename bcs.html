Child (Pediatric) Health Nursing

Child Health Nursing provides training to nurses who specialize in pediatric care, focusing on the medical and psychological needs of children and adolescents.

  • Neonatal Care
  • Childhood Immunization
  • Common Pediatric Diseases
  • Developmental Milestones
  • Pediatric Emergencies and Trauma

Community Health Nursing

Community Health Nursing focuses on health care delivery to underserved populations, health education, and disease prevention within a community.

  • Primary Health Care
  • Health Education and Promotion
  • Maternal and Child Health
  • Public Health Research
  • Community-Based Care

Medical Surgical Nursing

This department focuses on the care of patients undergoing surgery and their recovery, preparing nurses for various medical and surgical procedures.

  • Preoperative Care
  • Postoperative Care
  • Emergency Care
  • Surgical Nursing Techniques
  • Critical Care and Trauma

OBG Nursing

OBG Nursing focuses on maternal and child health, particularly prenatal, postnatal, and gynecological care, preparing nurses for delivery and reproductive health care.

  • Pregnancy and Childbirth Care
  • Gynecological Disorders
  • Family Planning
  • Neonatal Care
  • Women’s Health and Wellbeing

Mental (Psychiatric) Health Nursing

This department focuses on mental health care, equipping nurses with the skills to care for individuals experiencing psychiatric disorders.

  • Psychiatric Care and Therapy
  • Crisis Management
  • Mental Health Rehabilitation
  • Community Mental Health Care
  • Psychiatric Nursing Ethics

Nursing Foundation

The Nursing Foundation department provides the essential knowledge and skills needed for a career in nursing, including basic care and ethical practices.

  • Introduction to Nursing
  • Patient Care Fundamentals
  • Nursing Ethics and Communication
  • Medical Terminology
  • Health Assessment and Record Keeping
