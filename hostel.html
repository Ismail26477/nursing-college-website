Hostel Facilities

Our institution provides well-furnished hostel facilities for both boys and girls, ensuring a safe, secure, and homely environment. The hostels are equipped with all essential amenities including 24/7 electricity, water supply, Wi-Fi, study areas, and recreational spaces. Special attention is given to cleanliness, discipline, and nutritious meals. Wardens and caretakers are available around the clock to support and supervise the students, promoting a comfortable living and learning experience.
