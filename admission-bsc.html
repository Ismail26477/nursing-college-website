Admission Guidelines

Eligibility Criteria

  • The minimum age for admission shall be 17 years on 31st December of the year in which admission is sought.
  • Minimum education:
    • For OPEN Category: 10+2 class passed with Science (PCB) & English Core/English Elective with aggregate of 45% marks from recognized board under AISSCE/CBSE/ICSE/SSCE/HSCE or other equivalent Board.
    • For Reserved Category: 10+2 class passed with Science (PCB) & English Core/English Elective with aggregate of 40% marks from recognized board under AISSCE/CBSE/ICSE/SSCE/HSCE or other equivalent Board.
  • Students appearing in 10+2 examination in science conducted by National Institute of Open School with 45% marks.
  • Student shall be admitted once in a year.
  • Student shall be medically fit.
  • Have appeared for MH-B.Sc. Nursing CET exam.

Admission Process

DMER Process

Step 1 - Pass 12th with required criteria and appear for MH-B.Sc. Nursing CET
Step 2 - Register on www.mahacet.org and fill the preference list
Step 3 - Mahacet releases list of selected candidates
Step 4 - Complete college application, document scrutiny, and fee payment

Institutional Quota (Including NRI)

Step 1 - NRI quota advertisement is released
Step 2 - Fill the online application form
Step 3 - Merit list declared based on CET marks
Step 4 - Pay the fees and submit required documents

Documents Required

  • 3 sets of attested photocopies
  • 8 passport size photographs
  • MH CET B.Sc. Nursing Admit Card (if applicable)
  • MH CET B.Sc. Nursing mark sheet
  • Domicile/Nationality Certificate
  • S.S.C. Mark Sheet & Passing Certificate
  • H.S.C. Mark Sheet & Passing Certificate
  • Medical Fitness Certificate (In MH CET Brochure format)
  • College Leaving Certificate
  • Gap Certificate (if applicable)
  • Aadhar Card (Attested photocopy)
  • For Reserved Category:
    • Caste Certificate
    • Caste Validity Certificate - Mandatory
    • Non-Creamy Layer Certificate (If Applicable)
  • For NRI Students:
    • Family tree Affidavit signed by claimant
    • Photocopy of sponsor & student passport
    • Relationship documents between sponsor and student
    • Affidavits disclosing full identity of claimant and sponsor
    • Utility bills/ITR confirming sponsor’s address
    • Last 6 months bank statement
    • Valid VISA of sponsor & student (if applicable)

Important Instructions

  • Fees for DMER CAP and Institutional quota to be paid at admission.
  • DMER CAP students must pay by A/c payee Demand Draft to Dr. R.N. Lahoti College of Nursing, Sultanpur.
  • Institutional quota students must pay via NEFT.
  • Fees for subsequent academic years must be paid as per schedule.
  • Disclaimer: Dr. R.N. Lahoti College of Nursing has no agents, representatives, or other admission offices outside Sultanpur. Do not deal with unauthorized persons or organizations.
